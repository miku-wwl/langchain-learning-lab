Northstar Shop internal FAQ (fictional)

Refund policy
Customers may request a refund for ordinary physical products within 7 calendar days after delivery.
Digital downloads, opened software licenses, and custom-made products are not eligible for change-of-mind refunds.
Support reviews refund requests within 2 business days.
After approval, the refund returns to the original payment method in 3 to 5 business days.
For defective products, Northstar Shop pays the return shipping cost.

Delivery
Standard orders are delivered in 2 to 4 business days after dispatch.
Remote-area deliveries can take up to 6 business days after dispatch.
Customers receive a tracking link by email when an order is dispatched.

Membership
Members receive a 10 percent discount on eligible physical products.
Gift cards and shipping fees do not receive the membership discount.

Support
Customer support is available at support@northstar.example from Monday to Friday.
Support replies to email within 1 business day.
